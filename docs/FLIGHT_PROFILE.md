# SpaceX Starship V3 Flight Profile & Trajectory Timeline

This document details the multi-phase flight event timeline for both the Starship Upper Stage and Super Heavy Booster.

---

## Mission Timeline & Event Sequence

| Time (s) | Event | Description | Status / Key Parameters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T-00:00** | **Liftoff & Ignition** | 33 Raptor 3 engines ignite at 100% throttle ($90.6\,\text{MN}$). AGM engages at $\gamma = 1.20$. | Full Stack Liftoff ($5,500\,\text{t}$) |
| **T+00:15** | **Pitchover Maneuver** | Initial gravity turn pitchover begins toward 90° azimuth (Due East). | Pitch: $85^\circ$, Velocity: $250\,\text{m/s}$ |
| **T+00:32** | **Max-Q Throttle Notch** | Dynamic pressure peaks at $q = 64.2\,\text{kPa}$. Engines throttle back by 30% to 70%. | Structural loads suppressed |
| **T+00:55** | **100% Throttle Re-engagement** | Vehicle clears dense atmosphere ($z > 15\,\text{km}$). Throttle restored to 100%. | Acceleration increases to $3.5\,\text{g}$ |
| **T+01:40** | **Hot-Staging Separation** | Soviet-style hot staging. 5 center booster engines hold fuel; 6 Upper Stage Raptors ignite. | Plume impingement on booster dome |
| **T+01:42** | **Booster RCS 180° Flip** | Super Heavy activates cold-gas RCS thrusters to rotate $180^\circ$ retrograde. | Angular rate: $45^\circ/\text{s}$ |
| **T+01:45** | **Booster Boostback Burn** | 13 center Raptor 3 engines fire for 38 seconds to reverse downrange speed toward $(0,0)$. | Velocity: $v_x \to -320\,\text{m/s}$ |
| **T+02:20** | **PEG Closed-Loop Guidance** | Starship Upper Stage engages 1.0s iterative Powered Explicit Guidance (PEG). | Linear Tangent Steering active |
| **T+04:10** | **Booster Grid-Fin Entry Glide** | 4 titanium grid fins stabilize Super Heavy during hypersonic and supersonic atmospheric entry. | Glide angle of attack: $15^\circ$ |
| **T+05:47** | **SECO & Orbit Insertion** | Starship cuts off engines upon achieving circular velocity of $7,755\,\text{m/s}$ at $301.7\,\text{km}$. | 11.0% Reserve Fuel Remaining |
| **T+05:48** | **Payload Deployment** | Gold satellite with dual solar arrays deployed from Starship nose bay into stable orbit. | Mission Success |
| **T+05:55** | **Booster Landing & Catch** | 3 center Raptors ignite for landing burn. Booster hovers into Mechazilla launch tower arms. | Touchdown speed: $-2.0\,\text{m/s}$ at $(0,0)$ |
