💨 TRANSONIC CFD AERODYNAMICS & MAX-Q ANALYSIS

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulation across subsonic, transonic, supersonic, and hypersonic regimes (Mach 0.0 to Mach 25.0).

📊 Transonic Drag Rise & Shockwaves

At Mach 1.05, wave drag peaks at $C_d = 0.68$ due to the bow shock on the Starship nose cone and detached oblique shocks on the forward flaps. The simulation activates a 30% throttle reduction (down to 70% throttle) between $T+25\,\text{s}$ and $T+42\,\text{s}$ to cap dynamic pressure at $32.0\,\text{kPa}$.

Mach 0.8:  Cd = 0.28 (Subsonic)

Mach 3.0:  Cd = 0.38 (Supersonic Decay)
Mach 25.0: Cd = 0.26 (Hypersonic Asymptote)

🔥 Thermal Protection System (TPS) Gradient

Hexagonal ceramic tiles protect the windward belly of Starship during orbital re-entry from $1,600^\circ\text{C}$ plasma flow. Reentry angle of attack is stabilized at $60^\circ$ transitioning to $70^\circ$ belly-flop before the final flip-and-catch maneuver.
